About This Community Guide

The first version of this guide was developed collaboratively by our student developers in Faitech and Tribes computers as a practical web-development project. Working together, they researched, designed and built the foundation of the community guide. Building on their work, V2 introduces a more refined and professional experience, with improved organization, navigation, responsiveness and presentation of community resources.
